About the shop

Maktabatul Ilm began with a simple idea: good books should be easy to find and fairly priced. We stock books in Arabic and English, with a focus on classical Arabic texts and their explanations.

Every book is checked before it is packed. If something is not as described, we will put it right.

Delivery

Orders are posted by Royal Mail within two working days. Most books arrive in two to four days. Delivery rates are shown at checkout.

Returns

Changed your mind? You have 14 days from delivery to return a book in the condition you received it.

Contact

Questions about a title or an order? Email hello@example.com. We reply within one working day.
